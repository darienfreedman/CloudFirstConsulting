Establish a right-sized Azure foundation

Rapid product delivery can outpace cloud ownership, security baselines, and spending visibility.

Approach

Design a proportionate Azure foundation with scoped identities, environment separation, policy, budgets, and recovery requirements.

Deliverables

  • Cloud foundation and deployment blueprint
  • Ownership, budget, and readiness criteria

Success measures: Baseline coverage, budget variance, and environment readiness.

Requirements to consider

Scale controls to risk without bypassing isolation, access review, or customer data obligations.

Protect customer data as the team grows

Early collaboration patterns and expanding access create gaps in customer-information handling.

Approach

Identify sensitive information and accountable owners, review access, and stage classification and sharing controls for supported systems.

Deliverables

  • Customer-data handling assessment
  • Access and protection-policy roadmap

Success measures: Excess permissions, owner coverage, and high-priority data gaps.

Requirements to consider

Do not claim certifications or compliance before appropriate assessment and evidence.

Framework connections

  • GDPRWhere customer-data processing falls within GDPR scope, establish roles, permitted purposes, retention, and rights handling before scaling.
  • ISO/IEC 27001:2022Define the management-system scope and ownership needed to sustain customer-data controls rather than treating a cloud configuration as certification.

Cloud & AI

Take an AI product beyond a prototype

A promising AI demo lacks a clear plan for data permissions, quality, reliability, and operating cost.

Approach

Define the product's permitted behavior, establish grounded context and scoped tools, and evaluate normal, denied, and failure cases before release.

Deliverables

  • AI application and control architecture
  • Evaluation, release, and operating requirements

Success measures: Task quality, safe failure, latency, and cost per useful interaction.

Requirements to consider

A prototype is not production assurance; consequential actions and customer-data use require explicit authorization.

Framework connections

  • EU AI ActWhere the Act applies, determine the startup's provider or deployer responsibilities, use classification, and effective obligations before placing the AI product into service.
  • NIST AI RMFMap intended use and affected people, evaluate limitations, and establish accountable release and monitoring decisions beyond the prototype.
