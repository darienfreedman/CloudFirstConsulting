Security

Protect client financial records

Financial records and working papers move between teams with inconsistent sharing controls.

Approach

Review client and engagement access, classify sensitive information, and introduce protection policies that match approved handling requirements.

Deliverables

  • Client-data access and protection review
  • Classification and sharing-control plan

Success measures: Excess access, protected repositories, and unresolved exceptions.

Requirements to consider

Maintain client confidentiality and let qualified owners determine retention and professional obligations.

Framework connections

  • GDPRWhere client personal-data processing falls within GDPR scope, document roles, lawful purposes, retention, and client-approved sharing.
  • ISO/IEC 27001:2022Link client-record risks, access ownership, and evidence of handling controls to the scoped information security management system.
