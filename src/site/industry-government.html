Review cross-agency and contractor access

Approach

Deliverables

  • External-access and sponsorship assessment
  • Review and deprovisioning runbooks

Success measures: Sponsor coverage and overdue access reviews.

Requirements to consider

  • CISA ZTMMUse identity governance and visibility to track sponsors, access reviews, and retirement of cross-agency permissions.
