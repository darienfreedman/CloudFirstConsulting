Website privacy

How information is handled while you explore our services and contact us.

Browsing and preferences

If you choose light or dark mode, that appearance preference is saved in your browser's local storage. Before you choose, the site follows your device's appearance setting. Clearing the saved preference restores that default.

Search terms, filters, and service-finder choices operate in page memory and are not submitted to the contact service. Clarity may observe interactions with these controls and rendered page content, subject to its masking settings.

Site assets are served with the website, while the Clarity integration loads resources from Microsoft. Hosting and network providers may process standard request information, such as Internet Protocol addresses and user agents, under their own policies. Your browser may retain history, downloaded files, or other information independently.

Inquiries and appointments

The Contact Us page embeds Forms. Opening that page loads content from Microsoft, which may process request information and use its own cookies under its privacy terms. Responses are submitted directly through Forms to the form owner, not copied to this website's contact application programming interface.

Microsoft manages the form's validation, confirmation, and response storage. Do not include credentials, sensitive customer records, or confidential project documents. Bookings links, when offered, also use Microsoft's service and privacy terms. Browsing and service-finder choices are not automatically transferred to either service.

External links

Products explained link to websites with their own privacy practices. This site's no-referrer policy prevents the originating web address from being sent with those navigations in supporting browsers.

Product availability, licensing, and technical requirements change. Confirm the supported capabilities for your environment before making deployment decisions. Scope, responsibilities, data handling, and commercial terms are agreed for each engagement.

Our approach to security and privacy

Clarity site and privacy policy disclosure

Site disclosure

We use Clarity, Microsoft's analytics service, to understand how visitors use this website and improve its content, navigation, and usability. This involves collecting and sharing website interaction and usage data with Microsoft. The details below explain the information involved and link to Microsoft's privacy practices.

Privacy policy disclosure

Clarity captures behavioral metrics, heatmaps, and session replays to help us understand which pages and services interest visitors and where the website could work better. Information can include page views, clicks, scrolling, and interactions with rendered page content. We use these insights to improve the website and how we present our services.

Clarity can use first-party and third-party cookies and other tracking technologies to collect website usage information and understand online activity.

Microsoft processes information collected through Clarity under its own privacy practices. For details about how Microsoft collects, uses, and protects data, read the Microsoft privacy statement. Do not enter credentials, confidential records, or other sensitive information into website search fields or interactive controls.
