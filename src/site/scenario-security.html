Solution example

From disconnected alerts
to a shared security picture.

The business challenge

New cloud workloads and collaboration services have expanded the estate. Existing tools produce alerts, but ownership is inconsistent and the team cannot easily distinguish critical gaps from routine noise. Leadership wants an actionable view of risk, not another isolated dashboard.

A proposed approach

Start with the important business applications and the information they handle. Review identity and device protection, relevant security alerts, and existing escalation paths. Connect the highest-priority business threats to a clear investigation and response plan.

Sequence improvements across Entra, Defender XDR, and Sentinel. Pilot detection changes, tune noisy rules, and document response decisions. Keep disruptive actions behind explicit authorization and preserve the operational team's recovery options.

Practical deliverables

  • An exposure inventory and prioritized risk register.
  • An identity and detection coverage assessment.
  • A sequenced improvement backlog with named responsibilities.
  • Response runbooks and a review cadence for remaining gaps.

Business goals

A clearer shared view of exposure, more consistent investigation, and better-defined response ownership. Baseline alert quality, triage time, and remediation backlog age before agreeing on targets.
