Establish release gates for financial AI applications

Teams lack consistent evidence for deciding whether an AI application is ready to release.

Approach

Define approved data sources, identity boundaries, evaluation datasets, and release criteria tied to the proposed application's consequences.

Deliverables

  • AI release-control architecture
  • Evaluation and operational readiness checklist

Success measures: Evaluated releases, unresolved risks, and task-quality acceptance.

Requirements to consider

Keep lending, investment, and other consequential financial decisions under authorized professional oversight.

Framework connections

  • EU AI ActWhere the Act applies, classify the intended financial use and provider or deployer role before approving release; professional oversight alone does not establish compliance.
  • NIST AI RMFMap the application's consequences, measure task quality and misuse risks, and record accountable release and monitoring decisions.
