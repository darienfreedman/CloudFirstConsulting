Govern a changing workforce's access

Role changes, temporary staff, and external collaborators complicate access management.

Approach

Map responsibilities to access, establish sponsorship and review, and stage stronger authentication and privileged-access controls.

Deliverables

Success measures: Overdue access reviews and unnecessary standing privilege.

Cloud & AI

Evaluate nonclinical research AI applications

Research support prototypes lack consistent data boundaries and evaluation evidence.

Approach

Design a bounded platform for approved nonclinical tasks with controlled sources, traceable evaluations, and reviewed release decisions.

Deliverables

  • Research-support AI architecture
  • Data-use and evaluation criteria

Success measures: Source validity, task quality, and unresolved release risks.

Requirements to consider

Respect study permissions and consent; exclude diagnosis and treatment recommendations from this scope.

Framework connections

  • EU AI ActWhere the Act applies, assess the specific research use and organizational role rather than assuming every research activity is exempt or every health-related assistant is high-risk.
  • NIST AI RMFEvaluate the approved nonclinical task, source limitations, and affected stakeholders before agreeing research-support release criteria.
