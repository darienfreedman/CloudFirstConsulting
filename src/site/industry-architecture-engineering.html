Security

Protect design and project information

Design files and project documents are shared across changing internal and external teams.

Approach

Map approved collaboration, identify information owners, and stage access, classification, and sharing improvements.

Deliverables

  • Project-information protection review
  • Classification and external-sharing plan

Success measures: Owner coverage and high-risk sharing exceptions.

Requirements to consider

Respect client contracts, intellectual-property rights, and approved project access boundaries.

Framework connections

  • CMMC 2.0If design information is subject to defense-contract requirements, establish the contractual CMMC boundary before sharing project repositories.
  • ISO/IEC 27001:2022Include project information ownership, approved sharing, and risk exceptions in the organization's defined management-system scope.

Govern AI-assisted project workflows

AI tools are added to project workflows without consistent ownership or approved action limits.

Approach

Review orchestration frameworks, Model Context Protocol connections, information access, and approval steps; define evaluations before expanding the workflow.

Deliverables

  • AI workflow and control design
  • Release and monitoring criteria

Success measures: Approved workflow coverage and unresolved control gaps.

Requirements to consider

Do not let AI approve designs, safety conclusions, or contractual changes on its own.

  • NIST AI RMFAssess the consequences of AI-assisted project work and verify approved tools, human review, and failure handling before release.
