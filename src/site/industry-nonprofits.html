Protect sensitive mission information

Small teams handle donor and beneficiary information across shared tools.

Approach

Identify essential data, minimize unnecessary access, and stage classification and sharing controls.

Deliverables

  • Data handling and exposure review
  • Prioritized protection plan

Success measures: Sensitive-data coverage and unresolved sharing gaps.

Requirements to consider

Respect donor preferences, beneficiary dignity, and applicable privacy requirements.

Framework connections

Cloud & AI

Plan continuity for donation and program systems

Important administrative platforms have unclear dependencies and recovery assumptions.

Approach

Assess a bounded portfolio and establish recovery, migration, and operational ownership criteria before changes.

Deliverables

  • Platform dependency and readiness assessment
  • Recovery and modernization roadmap

Success measures: Recovery exercise outcomes and unresolved continuity risks.

Requirements to consider

Protect donor and beneficiary data and authorize changes affecting public availability or financial processing.

Framework connections

  • NIST CSF 2.0Connect recovery priorities and approved migration decisions to continuity of donation and beneficiary services.
  • PCI DSS 4.0If donation systems process card payments or affect the cardholder data environment, confirm payment-provider responsibilities and recovery changes within PCI DSS scope.

Establish proportionate security incident readiness

Approach
