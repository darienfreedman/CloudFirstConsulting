Protect engineering collaboration

Design and supplier information cross organizational boundaries.

Approach

Review project access, external sharing, classification, and lifecycle controls for in-scope engineering repositories.

Deliverables

  • Intellectual property handling and access assessment
  • Classification and sharing-control plan

Success measures: Protection coverage and unresolved access exceptions.

Requirements to consider

Honor contractual, export-control, and intellectual-property requirements.

Framework connections

  • CMMC 2.0If engineering repositories contain information from defense contracts, identify the required CMMC scope and evidence before approving external collaboration.
  • ISO/IEC 27001:2022Connect engineering information risks and supplier-sharing controls to the organization's defined management-system scope.

Cloud & AI

Govern releases of business AI applications

Business AI prototypes lack consistent evaluation and operational release controls.

Approach

Define a bounded application platform with approved sources, scoped identities, evaluation datasets, and release evidence.

Deliverables

  • AI platform and integration architecture
  • Evaluation and readiness criteria

Success measures: Evaluated releases, task quality, and unresolved risks.

Requirements to consider

Exclude autonomous driving, vehicle control, and safety-critical decisions from this business-AI scope.

Framework connections

  • EU AI ActWhere the Act applies, assess the business application's intended use and provider or deployer role instead of assuming all automotive AI has the same classification.
  • NIST AI RMFUse consequence-based evaluation and accountable release decisions for the specific business AI workflow.
