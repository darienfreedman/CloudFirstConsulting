Create a bounded research-support AI platform

Research support prototypes lack consistent data permissions and release requirements.

Approach

Design an isolated application foundation using approved sources, scoped identities, evaluated outputs, and documented release decisions.

Deliverables

  • Research-support AI architecture
  • Data-use and evaluation criteria

Success measures: Evaluation coverage and unresolved data-access risks.

Requirements to consider

Respect research consent, licensing, and ethics review; exclude autonomous academic decisions.

Framework connections

  • EU AI ActWhere the Act applies, classify the actual research-support use and organizational role; do not assume that a research label removes all obligations.
