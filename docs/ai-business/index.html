Modern work and productivity

Help people work better.
Make Microsoft 365 count.

The modern work is the productivity foundation of AI Business Solutions. Make Microsoft 365 and Viva useful in everyday work: collaborating in Teams and Outlook, finding trusted information, learning new skills, and coordinating tasks without unnecessary handoffs.

We help align Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, Exchange, and Microsoft 365 apps with your business processes. Establish sensible workspace ownership, information organization, and adoption practices before adding more tools or expanding Copilot.

Use Viva Connections to bring news and resources together, Viva Engage to support employee communities, and Viva Learning to make development easier to access. For organizational Viva Insights, agree the business purpose, privacy safeguards, and appropriate access before using workforce measures.

Where a cloud desktop fits the need, plan Windows 365 Cloud PC provisioning and the employee experience. Coordinate device readiness and Intune management with the security requirements of your organization.

ExchangeIntuneMicrosoft 365OneDriveSharePointTeamsVivaWindows 365

What we address

  • Productivity discovery, licensing alignment, and underused Microsoft 365 capabilities.
  • Teams and SharePoint workspace design, document collaboration, search, and content ownership.
  • Viva employee communications, communities, learning, and privacy-aware adoption insights.
  • Email, calendar, meetings, and task-management practices that reduce avoidable coordination work.
  • Windows 365 readiness, provisioning, and access requirements for the selected edition.
  • Role-based training, adoption champions, support handover, and a foundation for Copilot.

A practical output

A Microsoft 365 and Viva productivity assessment, collaboration and employee-experience design, prioritized implementation plan, and adoption playbook.

Measures to agree: time to find information, task completion, avoidable handoffs, support demand, and sustained use of the agreed workflows. Confirm licensing and supported features before rollout.

Copilot readiness and enablement

Make everyday AI
ready for your business.

Microsoft 365 Copilot is an AI experience, not a single generative pre-trained transformer model. It combines models with your work context, application integrations, and permission-aware access. Governance depends on the controls your organization configures and operates, not just the model behind an answer.

Align the product and supported model options with your use cases, prepare the information it can access, and evaluate the current experience with a focused pilot.

What we address

  • Business use-case discovery and role-based pilot selection.
  • Product differences, supported model choices, and administrator settings.
  • Licensing, identity, device, and information-access readiness.
  • Oversharing risks, collaboration policies, and protection requirements.
  • Task-based evaluation, enablement materials, and rollout decisions.

A practical output

A readiness assessment, prioritized remediation actions, pilot charter, and adoption plan.

Measures to agree: readiness gaps closed, useful task completion, quality of outputs, and sustained adoption within the pilot group.

Business process transformation

Reduce the friction
between intent and action.

Reimagine a process before automating it. Identify repeated work, unnecessary handoffs, and decisions that need better context, then connect business applications and AI around a clear outcome.

Copilot StudioDynamics 365Power AppsPower Automate

Where to start

  • Sales: opportunity preparation, account context, and proposal workflows.
  • Service: knowledge retrieval, request triage, and guided case resolution.
  • Finance: document review, exception routing, and management reporting.
  • Operations: request fulfillment, process visibility, and approved system updates.

A practical output

A current-to-future process map, integration design, decision and approval matrix, and a scoped implementation backlog.

Measures to agree: cycle time, rework, handoff volume, exception rates, and service quality. Keep consequential decisions subject to agreed oversight.

Adoption and value realization

Make the change stick.
Make its value visible.

A deployment is a starting point, not the business outcome. Define how people will use the solution, how it will be supported, and what evidence is needed to justify the next investment.

Microsoft 365Power AppsPower AutomatePower BI

What we address

  • Stakeholder ownership, communications, and role-based enablement.
  • Pilot feedback, support processes, and workflow refinements.
  • Baselines and success criteria that balance value, quality, and risk.
  • Scale, pause, or redesign decisions based on evidence.

A practical output

An adoption playbook, measurement framework, support handover, and prioritized improvement plan.

Measures to agree: sustained use, process quality, avoidable rework, and stakeholder feedback. Time saved is not automatically a realized financial benefit.
