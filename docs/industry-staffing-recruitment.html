Security

Protect candidate and client records

Candidate information is copied across collaboration tools without consistent access or retention controls.

Approach

Identify approved uses and repositories, review permissions, and stage classification and protection policies with responsible owners.

Deliverables

  • Data handling and exposure assessment

Cloud and AI Platforms

Control AI tool connections to recruiting systems

AI assistants may reach sensitive records or take actions through overly broad integrations.

Approach

Review Model Context Protocol servers and multi-step workflows, restrict approved records and tools, and evaluate denial, failure, and escalation behavior.

Deliverables

  • AI integration and access design
  • Evaluation and release-control checklist

Success measures: Approved tools and unresolved permission gaps.

Requirements to consider

AI must not make hiring or rejection decisions or use sensitive attributes to determine candidate suitability.

Framework connections

  • EU AI ActWhere the Act applies, classify the intended recruiting use and organizational role before enabling tools; retain the explicit exclusion of AI hiring or rejection decisions.
  • NIST AI RMFTest denied actions, sensitive-record access, and escalation so a connected workflow cannot silently become an automated employment decision.
