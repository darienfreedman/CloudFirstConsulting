Before you get started

Frequently asked questions

What to consider when shaping a security, artificial intelligence, or cloud engagement.

Where does Viva fit into the modern workplace?

Viva complements Microsoft 365 with employee communications, communities, learning, and insights. We help identify useful experiences in Viva Connections, Engage, Learning, and Insights, then define content ownership, adoption, and privacy requirements. Product access and capabilities depend on licensing and configuration; measuring activity alone does not establish productivity.

Explore Microsoft 365 and Viva
Can Entra Suite replace our legacy VPN?

Entra Private Access provides Zero Trust Network Access for supported user-to-application scenarios, while Entra Internet Access provides an identity-aware secure web gateway for internet and software as a service traffic. These are security service edge capabilities within a broader secure access service edge architecture. Start with application discovery and a pilot, test compatibility and recovery, then retire eligible virtual private network (VPN) access in phases. Do not assume the same design replaces site-to-site connections or every legacy application requirement.

Explore secure access and VPN modernization
Is Copilot a model, or a governance layer?

Copilot is a family of artificial intelligence products and experiences, not a single model. Microsoft 365 Copilot combines models, work context, application integrations, and permission-aware access. Copilot Studio supports building agents with approved knowledge, tools, and model choices. These capabilities support governance, but do not replace your organization's policies, configuration, or accountability.

It is not locked to one launch-era generative pre-trained transformer model, but model choice is not unlimited. Availability depends on the specific product, feature, license, region, and administrator settings.

Understand Copilot, models, and governance
Should we reconsider Copilot if an earlier experience was disappointing?

Evaluate the current product on tasks that matter to your team. Models and capabilities evolve, but better results also depend on relevant sources, appropriate access, and clear instructions. Use a bounded pilot to measure accuracy, useful task completion, review effort, and safe handling of information. A newer model alone is not a reason to expand deployment.

How to evaluate the current experience
What is the difference between AI Security and AI Governance?

AI Security protects the information, identities, applications, and tools involved in using artificial intelligence (AI). AI Governance defines ownership, approved uses, review steps, and accountability. They work together: a policy needs practical controls, and those controls need an owner and a clear business purpose.

Explore AI Security and AI Governance
What are MCP servers and orchestration frameworks?

An Model Context Protocol (MCP) server connects an artificial intelligence application to tools or information using a shared protocol. An orchestration framework coordinates a workflow, including which agent or function acts next. Neither removes the need for access checks, approved data use, or review before important actions. We help you define and evaluate those requirements in the context of your business.

Where should we start if everything feels like a priority?

Start with a business-critical service, a material risk, or a clearly owned process. A focused assessment can establish the current state, identify dependencies, and separate immediate actions from longer-term change. You do not need a company-wide transformation program to make useful progress.

Can we build on the products we already own?

Yes. The approach begins with your existing investments, configuration, usage, and licensing. Identify underused capabilities and gaps before recommending additional products. A license does not by itself mean the capability has been configured, adopted, or made operational.

Does this mean replacing our team or current technology providers?

No. An engagement can focus on a defined gap, project, or workload and fit alongside existing teams and providers. Agree on responsibilities, integration boundaries, and the handover model during scoping. A Microsoft-centered architecture can still account for third-party systems and hybrid dependencies.

How should access to our data and environment be handled?

Define the minimum data and permissions required for the agreed work. Establish authorized identities, approved access paths, confidentiality requirements, and change approvals before accessing any environment. Use redacted or synthetic information where practical. Do not enter credentials or sensitive information into this website; it does not collect or connect to your systems.

What determines the timeline and cost?

Scope, estate size, licensing, data readiness, integration complexity, access approvals, and stakeholder availability all affect the plan. Establish prerequisites and acceptance criteria first, then agree on milestones and commercial terms.

Can these services support regulated environments?

The design can incorporate requirements for access, retention, audit evidence, and data handling. Your legal, privacy, and compliance stakeholders must identify applicable obligations and approve interpretations. A configured product or completed assessment is not a certification or a guarantee of compliance.

What happens after an assessment or pilot?

Review the evidence against the agreed success criteria. Decide whether to implement, expand, refine, or stop. An implementation phase should include operational ownership, training, support procedures, and a review cadence, not just a technical deployment.

Ongoing optimization is a separate scope to define around posture, adoption, reliability, or cost.
